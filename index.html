Our story

From the river, with love

Nile House began as a family kitchen in Zamalek, feeding neighbours through open windows. Today our chefs rework Egyptian heritage dishes with modern technique, local farms and a wood fire that never goes out.

Everything is made in house: the baladi bread, the tahini stone-ground each morning, the molokhia picked at dawn. Come hungry, leave family.
